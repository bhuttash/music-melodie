What I Do

I’ve been a private teacher for over a year. I enjoy it so much. It started with a homeschooling mom, who referred me to her homeschooling friends, and my school keeps growing!

I enjoy connecting with my students, learning a little about them, what they want out of the lessons and what goals they (or moms 😉) want for them.
