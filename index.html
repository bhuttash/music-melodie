What I Do

I’ve been a private teacher for over a year. I enjoy it so much. It started with a homeschooling mom, who referred me to her homeschooling friends, and my school keeps growing!

I enjoy connecting with my students, learning a little about them, what they want out of the lessons and what goals they (or moms 😉) want for them.

My Work

I’ve been teaching music to my own kids for close to 12 years. My kids still at home are ages 12 and 17. They are here for such a short time; a realization made after my oldest (now 21) went on a church mission for 2 years.

I started teaching private lessons over a year ago. These pictures are some of my current students. I know people have varied learning styles that work for them. Through my experiences, I have observed this first-hand, and I make an effort to cater my lessons to how each student learns best. Some are visual, others verbal, and some both.

I’ve been very blessed to go from five homeschooling students in the begging to about 15 students with a mix of homeschool and traditional students. More than half have started in the past 6 months. Thanks to some wonderful referrals from awesome moms, I now have triple the students I used to have.

With new students comes new ways of adapting and making room for my students and my home life. I try not to have the two converge, but sometimes it is hard to achieve balance when you have a family and children at home who need you.
